# SOP-EX-05 Travel on slopes and site traffic

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This procedure covers travelling the excavator on slopes and uneven ground, and following the site traffic rules.

## 2. Slopes and uneven ground

### 2.1 Check before you travel
Check the ground and the slope before you travel. Do not travel on slopes steeper than the limit in the OMM or the site plan, and avoid soft ground, loose material and edges.

### 2.2 Travel straight up or down
Travel straight up or straight down a slope. Avoid travelling across a slope, and avoid turning on a slope, because both increase the risk of sliding or tipping.

### 2.3 Keep the attachment low
When travelling, carry the bucket or attachment low and close to the ground, with the boom and stick pulled in. A raised attachment moves the centre of gravity up and makes the machine less stable.

### 2.4 No swinging on slopes
Do not swing the upper structure while on a slope unless the task requires it and the ground has been checked. Swinging on a slope can shift the load downhill.

### 2.5 Slow speed
Use low travel speed on slopes and uneven ground, and move smoothly without sudden stops.

## 3. Site traffic

### 3.1 Traffic plan and speed limits
Follow the site traffic plan, the marked travel routes and the posted speed limits for each zone.

### 3.2 Right of way
Follow the site right-of-way rules. Haul trucks and light vehicles must be able to see you before you enter a haul road.

### 3.3 Near people
Travel at walking pace or slower near people and in congested areas, and stop if anyone comes close to the machine.
