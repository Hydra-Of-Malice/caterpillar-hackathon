# SOP-GEN-01 Seatbelt and cab entry

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This procedure covers climbing into and out of the cab, wearing the seatbelt and parking the machine safely before leaving the operator seat.

## 2. Entering and leaving the cab

### 2.1 Three points of contact
Use three points of contact when climbing into or out of the cab: two hands and one foot, or two feet and one hand, on the steps and handholds at all times.

### 2.2 Facing the machine
Face the machine when you climb up or down. Keep the steps and handholds clean and free of mud, grease and ice, and report any damaged step or handhold.

### 2.3 Never jump
Never jump from the machine or the tracks. Do not carry tools or bags in your hands while climbing; pass them up or down separately.

## 3. Seatbelt

### 3.1 When to wear the seatbelt
Fasten the seatbelt before starting the engine and wear it at all times while the machine is operating, travelling or working. The seatbelt keeps you in the seat and inside the cab if the machine tips or stops suddenly.

### 3.2 Checking the seatbelt
Check the seatbelt at every pre-start inspection. It must latch and release correctly and must not be cut, frayed or worn. Report a damaged seatbelt and do not operate the machine until it is replaced.

### 3.3 Seatbelt alert
If the seatbelt alert appears while the machine is moving, stop all motion and fasten the seatbelt before continuing.

## 4. Leaving the seat: safe park

### 4.1 Lower the attachment
Before leaving the operator seat, bring the machine to a stop on level ground where possible and lower the bucket or attachment to the ground.

### 4.2 Hydraulic lockout
Engage the hydraulic lockout (move the hydraulic lock lever to the locked position) before you unfasten the seatbelt or leave the seat. With the lockout engaged, the controls cannot move the machine or the attachment if they are bumped.

### 4.3 Park brake and swing lock
Apply the park brake and the swing lock where fitted, following the machine's OMM.

### 4.4 Leaving the machine unattended
Before leaving the machine unattended, complete the safe-park steps, let the engine cool down if required, stop the engine and remove the key. Use three points of contact to climb down.
