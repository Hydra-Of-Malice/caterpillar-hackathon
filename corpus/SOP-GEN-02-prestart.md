# SOP-GEN-02 Pre-start inspection and walk-around

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This procedure describes the pre-start inspection and walk-around that must be completed before operating the machine at the start of each shift.

## 2. Walk-around

### 2.1 Around the machine
Walk all the way around the machine. Look for fluid leaks, damaged hoses, cracked or loose parts, damage to the tracks or tyres, worn or missing bucket teeth, and loose pins on the boom, stick and bucket.

### 2.2 Fluid levels
Check engine oil, coolant, hydraulic oil and fuel levels as described in the OMM for the daily checks.

### 2.3 Cab checks
Check that the windows, mirrors and cameras are clean and undamaged, that the seatbelt latches and is in good condition, and that the fire extinguisher is present and charged.

### 2.4 Work area
Check the work area before starting: people nearby, overhead power lines, soft or uneven ground and the position of trenches and edges.

## 3. Start-up checks

### 3.1 Gauges and warning lights
After starting the engine, check the gauges, warning lights and any active fault codes on the display. Record any fault code on the checklist.

### 3.2 Horn, alarms and lights
Test the horn, the travel alarm and the lights.

### 3.3 Controls and lockout
With the area clear, check that the controls respond normally and that the hydraulic lockout stops the controls from moving the machine when it is engaged.

## 4. Recording and defects

### 4.1 Complete the checklist first
Complete the pre-start checklist in CAT Sentinel before operating the machine. The shift cannot start until every item has been answered.

### 4.2 Critical defects
If you find a critical defect, such as a seatbelt fault, a brake or steering problem or a major leak, do not operate the machine. Tag it out, record the defect and report it to the supervisor.

### 4.3 Take the time needed
Do not rush the inspection. A walk-around done in a hurry misses defects.
